Kiara Feeding Summary

Summary of the complete chat about how much food to give Kiara at each meal.

Kiara’s known information

11.7 lbApproximate body weight
Adult dogApproximately 3 years old

Food shown

The photos show a 5.5 oz (156 g) can of Purina Beneful Gravy Wet Dog Food, steak flavor with green beans. The label identifies it as complete adult dog food.

Label-based amount

The visible feeding guide says to feed approximately 1 can per 4½–5½ lb of body weight per day.

For Kiara at 11.7 lb, that works out to about 2¼ to 2½ cans per day.

When feeding twice daily

A practical starting amount is about 1⅛ cans per meal, twice a day.

Important adjustment

  • Use the full amount only when this wet food is her main food.
  • Reduce the wet-food portion if she also receives kibble, table food, chews, or substantial treats.
  • Keep treats to roughly 10% or less of her total daily calories.
  • Adjust the portion if her weight or body condition begins to change.

How to judge whether the amount is right

  • You should be able to feel her ribs easily under a thin layer of fat.
  • She should have a visible waist when viewed from above.
  • If she gains weight, reduce portions slightly; if she loses weight unintentionally, increase them slightly and consider asking her veterinarian.

The earlier dry-food estimate of roughly ⅓–½ cup per meal was a generic estimate for kibble and does not apply directly to this canned food.
