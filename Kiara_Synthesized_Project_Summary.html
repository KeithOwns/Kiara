Kiara ("Kiki") - Synthesized Project Summary

Date: August 11, 2026

This document synthesizes multiple chat summaries concerning the adoption, care, health, and lifestyle of Kiara (also known as Kiki), a small mixed-breed rescue dog.

1. Identity & Background

  • Basic Stats: Adult female dog, approximately 3 years old, weighing 11.7 lbs.
  • Adoption: Adopted from West Valley Animal Shelter on July 14, 2026. She quickly settled into her new home, showing comfort and trust through belly-up sleeping positions.
  • Web Project: A responsive photo and video webpage titled "Kiara & Keith" was created to document their relationship, adoption story, and everyday life.

2. Breed & DNA Guesses

While visual identification is not definitive, several key traits suggest a specific mix:

  • Dachshund Influence: Suggested by her long torso, short legs, low carriage, and overall size.
  • Spaniel/Bichon Influence: Her soft, wavy, flowing coat, floppy feathered ears, and brown-and-white coloring strongly suggest Havanese, Shih Tzu, Maltese, Cavalier King Charles Spaniel, or Cocker Spaniel ancestry.
  • DNA Testing: While Embark Breed ID or Wisdom Panel Essential are recommended for reliable results, the decision was made not to DNA test Kiara, as it would not change her most important preventive care.

3. Veterinary & Health Monitoring

Orthopedic Concerns

Left Hind Leg Pop: A reproducible pop is felt/heard when Kiara's left hind leg reaches full extension. This could be a luxating patella or tendon issue and requires a veterinary orthopedic exam.

Reproductive & Skin Observations

  • Spay Tattoo: A faint blue-green line on her belly is confirmed to be a standard shelter spay tattoo.
  • Mammary/Vulva: Slightly prominent nipples and normal vulva size for a spayed female. No obvious signs of concern.
  • Ear Care: Brown crust deep in the ear was noted. Recommendation is to clean only the visible outer ear with dog-safe cleaner and schedule a vet check to rule out yeast or mites.
  • Paws & Skin: Rough paw pads are normal. Avoid human foot creams. For potential dandruff near the tail base, do not use human shampoo/conditioner. Use dog-formulated hypoallergenic shampoo.

4. Safety & Handling

Proper Lifting Technique

To protect her potentially vulnerable spine and hips:

  • Two-hand pickup: Support the chest behind the front legs and the pelvis/hindquarters simultaneously.
  • One-arm carry: Support her body/pelvis with your forearm, letting her front and hind legs gently saddle the arm. Do not let the legs dangle or extend straight backward.

Jumping & Swimming

  • Jumping: Use a non-slip ramp or pet steps for furniture and cars. Discourage jumping off elevated surfaces to protect her spine.
  • Swimming: Supervise continuously around the pool. Teach her where the exit/steps are, and consider a canine flotation vest.
  • Tracking: A Samsung Galaxy SmartTag2 is attached to her collar as a supplementary location-safety measure.

5. Diet & Grooming

  • Feeding: She is fed Purina Beneful Gravy Wet Dog Food (steak flavor with green beans) in 5.5 oz cans. Weight monitoring is a priority to keep her lean.
  • Grooming: Kiara received her first "summer cut" at home, which helps keep her coat clean and reduces matting. She is very tolerant of handling and bathing.
  • Dental: Small dogs need regular tooth brushing and routine dental checks.

6. Outings & Activities

  • Running: Because of the left-hind-leg pop and back vulnerability, running is not recommended until cleared by a vet. Begin with brisk walks and short jog intervals on level terrain.
  • Dog-Friendly Beaches: Leo Carrillo State Park (North Beach) is the primary traditionally dog-friendly beach in the Malibu/Marina del Rey area. However, running on soft sand places extra demand on her hips and knees and should be avoided until her leg is evaluated.
