The beginning

She came home.
Then she became home.

On July 14, 2026, Keith adopted Kiara from the West Valley Animal Shelter. She was about three years old, 11.7 pounds, and ready to become part of everything.

The days that followed quickly established a rhythm: Kiara travels close, settles in fast, watches everything, and turns ordinary errands into small adventures. Whether the setting is a car, a rooftop, or a neighborhood stop, she stays near Keith and makes herself completely at home.

The partnership

Not just along
for the ride.

Kiara does not simply accompany Keith. She participates: observing the room, greeting people, choosing the best seat, and occasionally deciding that the most comfortable place is directly in someone’s arms.

These photographs are not a polished campaign. They are the first pages of a relationship taking shape in real time.
